# Recap emails

Replace brackets. Send the same day. Do not start the extra work first.
Attach or paste numbers from `desk/loss-calculator.py --lookup …`.

## A — Soft recap

Subject: Recap — [project], [date]

Hi [name] —

Quick recap from [call/Slack] so we stay aligned.

Original scope: [one line].
New request: [one line, their words].

I can fold a small version into the current fee, or price it as a change. I’ll send the one-pager if it needs extra hours.

Reply “looks right” or correct me today.

[your name]

Subject: Change order — [short name] — $[fee]

Hi [name] —

You asked for [request]. That sits outside [original deliverable].

To add it:

- Extra hours: [n] (desk mid was [bench])

[your name]

Pausing anything beyond [original deliverable] until we confirm.

Requested extra: [one line].
If we take it: $[fee], new date [date].
